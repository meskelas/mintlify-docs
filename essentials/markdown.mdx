## **Privacy Relating to the Treatment of Personal Information**

- Direct messages ought to be a place of guaranteed privacy and confidentiality, unless that user is directly or indirectly violating Discord’s Terms of Service or violating EEO policy. You must ask for permission to share the professional conversation that has occurred; if they deny that request, you must obey. Conversations that take place through the ticket system will be logged and may be shared with other staff with a higher level of authority without prior permission.
