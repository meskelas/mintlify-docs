## **Intellectual Property and Security**

All intellectual property developed by staff members during their employment with Haunt, including discoveries or inventions made in the performance of their duties related in any way to the business of Haunt, will remain the property of Haunt.

Staff members may be given access to confidential information, data or any other server-related property/information in the performance of their duties. This must be protected and used only in the interests of Haunt.

**Staff members must not**:

- Disclose or use any part of any confidential information outside of the performance of their duties, and in the interests of Haunt.
- Authorize or be involved in the improper use or disclosure of confidential information.

<Warning>
  This includes, but is not limited to: sharing documents, unreleased updates, forms, and messages from staff channels.
</Warning>
