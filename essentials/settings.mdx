## **Team Regulations**

Our Team Regulations policies apply to all staff members and provide the framework of principles for conducting daily operations, dealing with other staff, partners, and public members. 

All staff members are responsible for their actions. We expect you to be able to handle situations appropriately and to be able to use your own judgement.  Staff members found in breach of this policy may be subject to disciplinary action.

#### **This policy is based on the following:**

1. Document actions properly. If an action is taken against a public member, that action requires a reason and cannot be deleted without a reason. Document proofs via the `;case create` command.
2. Follow the Discord Terms of Service and any applicable Haunt policies.
3. Exercise professionalism, fairness, equality, and sensitivity in dealing with other staff members, partners, and public members.
4. Avoid discussions revolved around sensitive, political, and controversial topics, as defined in the sensitive topics rule.
5. Keep Direct Messages open at all times for haunts support server.
6. Your About Me, custom pronouns, and Discord Status may not include anything sexually inappropriate, discriminatory, sensitive, or any [discord.gg](http://discord.gg) links (does not apply to ‘About Me’ bio)
7. Staff channels are private. Sharing content from these channels will result in immediate action. 
8. Run commands such as `;kick`, `;ban`, `;warn` & `;case create` in the [<u>staff-commands</u>](https://discord.com/channels/1363969049700073663/1453106780274954443) channel.
9. Mentioning roles or making announcements without prior authorization from the owner or a manager could result in action based on the context.
