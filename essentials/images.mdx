## **Resignation Procedure**

Eligible staff members have the ability to request a break whenever needed. You must provide a legitimate reason for your break request; unless that reason is too personal, then you may mark it as ‘personal’, but a member of HR may get in contact. HR can deny and approve break requests with a reason. Staff members are eligible to request a break if:

- They’ve worked for Haunt for 14 days or longer (unless there’s an emergency)
- They will remain inactive for longer than 48 hours.
- If we notice activity from you during your break, we may remove that break.

**To request a break**, go to the #staff-info channel and click the break request link, which will then take you to the break request form that will ask you for the duration and reason of your break. This will then be approved or denied by a manager.

**To end your break**, contact a manager. 

**To resign**, staff members are to use the resignation form found in the #staff-info channel. Then you must:

- Complete the form properly and submit it.
- Once submitted, <u>wait</u> for a manager or an HR Team member to sort roles. Failure to do so will lead to being blacklisted.
