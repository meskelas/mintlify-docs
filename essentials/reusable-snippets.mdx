## **Policy**

This policy applies to all staff and covers all work-related functions and activities. It also applies to all recruitment, selection, and promotion decisions.

**The objective of Haunt’s Equal Opportunity Policy is to improve server success by:**

- Attracting and retaining the best possible employees.
- Providing a safe, respectful, and flexible work environment.
- Delivering our services in a safe, respectful, and reasonably flexible way.

## **Discrimination & Sexual Harassment**

Haunt is committed to providing a workplace free from discrimination, sexual harassment, and bullying. Behavior that constitutes discrimination, sexual harassment, or bullying will not be tolerated and will lead to action being taken, which may include termination.

Haunt provides equal opportunity in employment to people without discrimination based on a personal characteristic protected under state and federal equal opportunity legislation.

Any staff members found to have contravened this policy will be subject to disciplinary action, which may include dismissal as outlined in the complaint procedure below.

Staff members must report any behavior that constitutes sexual harassment, bullying, or discrimination to a manager.

<Tip>
  Staff members will not be victimized or treated unfairly for raising an issue or making a complaint.
</Tip>

### How to Make a Complaint

If you believe you are being, or have been, discriminated against, sexually harassed or bullied, you should follow this procedure.

Tell the offender the behavior is offensive, unwelcome, and against server policy and should stop (only if you feel comfortable enough to approach them directly, speak to a manager). Keep a record of the incident(s).

Staff members should feel confident that any complaint they make is to be treated as confidentially as possible and will not be disclosed to anyone outside of the Human Resources team.

### Possible Outcomes

If, after investigation, HR finds the complaint is justified, HR will discuss with the complainant the appropriate outcomes, which may include:

1. Disciplinary action to be taken against the perpetrator. (counselling, strike, warning or dismissal)
2. Staff training.
3. Additional training for the perpetrator or all staff, as appropriate.
4. Counselling for the complainant.
5. An apology.
