## **Performance Improvement Policy**

Should a staff member's performance not reach our expectations, a manager may decide to end the staff member's employment. Depending on the circumstances, performance improvement action may include verbal or written warnings or strikes.

Each staff member must understand their responsibilities, be counselled, and given the opportunity to reach the standards expected of them. Haunt will give a staff member the opportunity to defend themselves before a manager or leadership takes further action.

**Note**<u>:</u> If staff members have a disability that requires reasonable adjustments to be made to the server to allow them to work safely and productively, they should raise this with a manager. Haunt will only refuse such requests on reasonable business grounds.
