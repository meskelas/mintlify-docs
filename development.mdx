## **Rank Info**

> Below are in-depth cards that display our chain-of-command (CoC). You can use them to figure out what your tasks are, or if you would like to know the structure of the roles. Note that ownership/development is not listed as it's not obtainable.

<Card title="Management" color="#2ecc71" icon="user-gear">
  The responsibilities of the Manager are to manage the server’s resources, such as updating guides. employee relations, policy enforcement, and discipline.
</Card>

<Card title="Leadership" color="#1f8b4c" icon="user-magnifying-glass">
  The responsibilities of the advisor are to handle external partnerships with popular websites/platforms and oversee the beta team.
</Card>

<Card title="Moderation" color="#76a8f3" icon="user-shield">
  The responsibilities of the Moderator are to enforce the server rules, make sure the chats are clean, and assist members in the support forum.
</Card>

<Card title="Support" color="#c8bcde" icon="user-tag">
  The responsibilities of the Helper are to assist members with any issues related to haunt, and solve posts in the support forum.
</Card>
