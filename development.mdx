## Rank Info

- **Ownership**: The owner, who is also the Developer of Haunt, is in charge of managing the overall operations and resources of the entire server and bot.
- **Management**: The responsibilities of the Manager are to manage the server’s resources, such as updating guides. employee relations, policy enforcement, and discipline.
- **Moderation**: The responsibilities of the Moderator are to enforce the server rules, make sure the chats are clean, and assist members in the support forum.
- **Support**: The responsibilities of the Support are to assist members with any issues related to haunt, and solve posts in the support forum.
