## **Staff Activity**

> All staff members are required to engage with the community on a regular basis. Each staff role has a different set of tasks they must follow to keep their position within the team. Please review the snippets of tasks below. A more in-depth version of the tasks will be listed in ranks.

<Card title="Management" color="#2ecc71" icon="user-gear" horizontal>
  _Engage in the chat, manage staff relations, and server operations._
</Card>

<Card title="Leadership" color="#1f8b4c" icon="user-magnifying-glass" horizontal>
  _Engage in the chat, overlook the beta team, and partnership requests._
</Card>

<Card title="Moderation " color="#76a8f3" icon="user-shield" horizontal>
  _Engage in the chat, moderate rule breakers, and assist with support threads_.
</Card>

<Card title="Support" color="#c8bcde" icon="user-pen" horizontal>
  _Engage in the chat and assist with support threads._
</Card>
