## **Welcome to the Team**

We are excited that you have decided to join us and look forward to a long, happy, and successful partnership together. Our server is primarily about providing support and announcing updates about Haunt. We also focus on community engagement through giveaways and chatting with the members.

The purpose of this handbook is to introduce you to our policies and procedures regarding our expectations around your behavior. If you have any questions about the content, please do not hesitate to contact a manager or the owner.

We want our staff members to sincerely enjoy their time at Haunt. This means that we will go through efforts to ensure your well-being isn’t compromised. We believe that personal development is important for our Team Members, and so we will provide you with resources that you can use to further develop your knowledge in the interests you share with Managers and Human Resources officers.

If you have any suggestions, feel free to Direct Message a manager or owner to provide your valuable feedback!
